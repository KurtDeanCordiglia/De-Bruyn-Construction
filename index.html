About us

Built properly, the first time.

We're a hands-on building company. The person who quotes your job is the person who runs it on site.

De Bruyn Developments & Projects takes on residential and light commercial work across the Western Cape. We keep the team small enough to stay close to every build and experienced enough to handle everything from foundations to finishes.

  • Plans, approvals and engineer coordination
  • Our own trusted subcontractor teams
  • Transparent variations, signed off before work starts
  • Clean, safe and secure sites
